# Broad Options Media Vision

## Purpose

Broad Options Media (BOMedia) requires a custom operating system to manage our internal operations with speed, clarity, and confidence.

The application must reduce manual work, eliminate duplicate record keeping, and provide our team with actionable business strategy insights for our indoor and outdoor advertising services.

---

# Long-Term Vision

The BOMedia internal platform will become an AI-assisted Business Operating System.

It will combine:

- Daily sales data entry
- Quotations
- Invoicing
- Customer management
- Inventory tracking (including flex banners, SAV, clear stickers, and window graphics)
- Expenses
- Production scheduling for the 10ft printing machine
- Payments
- Reports
- Analytics
- Business Intelligence

into one fast, beautiful, reliable application.

The experience should feel closer to:

- Google Wallet
- Stripe Dashboard
- Monzo
- Revolut
- Notion

than to traditional accounting software.

---

# Product Principles

## Fast

Every common action should require as few taps as possible.

The application should always feel responsive.

---

## Beautiful

The interface should be calm, modern and professional.

Use whitespace instead of clutter.

Avoid unnecessary colours.

Prioritise typography and spacing.

---

## Simple

Users should understand every screen within three seconds.

Never overwhelm users with unnecessary information.

Progressive disclosure should be used throughout the application.

---

## Consistent

Every screen should follow the same design language.

Buttons, cards, spacing, typography and colours should never feel different between screens.

---

## Reliable

The application should preserve user data, prevent accidental mistakes and provide clear feedback after every action.

---

# Target Users

Primary users include:

- BOMedia internal management
- Pre-press and production specialists
- Sales and administrative staff

The product must remain simple enough for our team to log daily sales and track large-format production without technical friction.

---

# Design Philosophy

Less information.

More clarity.

Better decisions.

Professional appearance.

Fast workflows.

Modern Material Design 3.

---

# User Experience Goals

The application must help our team:

- Record daily sales data quickly
- Issue accurate quotations for large-format printing jobs
- Generate professional invoices
- Track outstanding payments
- Manage print material inventory
- Monitor operational expenses
- Understand BOMedia's overall business performance
- Execute better business strategies based on real data

without needing accounting expertise.

---

# Engineering Philosophy

Build reusable components.

Avoid duplication.

Optimise for maintainability.

Prefer composition over repetition.

Keep features modular.

Follow the project Design System.

---

# AI Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence, including natural language data logging, must assist our team rather than replace them.

Examples include:

- Payment reminders
- Business strategy insights
- Pricing suggestions for custom print dimensions
- Material stock predictions
- Profit analysis
- Sales forecasting

AI must always explain recommendations clearly.

---

# Success Criteria

The application is successful when:

- Team members can complete daily sales data entry quickly
- The interface remains consistent
- Performance remains smooth with large datasets
- New features integrate naturally
- Maintenance remains straightforward
- The application becomes the ultimate daily operating tool for Broad Options Media
